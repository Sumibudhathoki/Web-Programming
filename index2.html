About the Book

Nilo Prem is a deeply emotional Nepali novel that explores love, longing, and the silent struggles of human relationships. The story beautifully portrays how love can be both comforting and painful, leaving a lasting impact on the reader’s heart.

About the Author

Purushottam Bajagai is a renowned Nepali writer known for his poetic storytelling and emotional depth. His works often reflect realism, human emotions, and social experiences.
